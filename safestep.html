Project background

SafeStep is a course project exploring a low-cost sensing insole for partial weight-bearing rehabilitation after lower-extremity surgery. The goal is to estimate affected-foot loading during walking and provide audio feedback when loading falls outside a clinician-prescribed range.

The engineering question is how to obtain useful foot-load estimates with a small number of sensors while keeping the system affordable, thin, portable, and practical to wear. The design treats measurement accuracy, repeatability, comfort, power consumption, and cost as linked design constraints.

System concept

The concept combines an eight-point sensor insole, acquisition electronics, data processing, and audio feedback to:

  • Capture plantar pressure signals during each step and estimate affected-foot loading.
  • Identify underload, target load, and overload relative to a configured loading range.
  • Provide immediate audio feedback and record loading history for patient and clinician review.

Team lead — sensor integration and hardware bring-up

I lead the team and am responsible for sensor integration, procurement and BOM preparation, hardware bring-up, calibration, and data streaming for the team’s load-estimation work.

Sensor selection and prototype planning

I reviewed resistive, capacitive, and strain-gauge sensor options and worked on requirements for an eight-point prototype. The prototype plan pairs a ZNX-01 sensor insole with ESP32 acquisition hardware and an initial BOM.

Hardware decisions and tradeoffs

Sensor count and calibration effort

I considered denser layouts alongside an eight-point configuration. Fewer points reduce wiring and processing demands but provide less spatial detail.

Electronics placement

Internal electronics provide a compact package. External electronics trade extra wiring for reduced thickness and exposure to repeated bending inside the shoe.

Cost and measurement quality

Calibration and repeatability are central to estimating force with low-cost sensors.

Hardware and software interface

Teammates need interpretable, consistently formatted sensor readings. Calibration and data streaming are part of the integration task.
